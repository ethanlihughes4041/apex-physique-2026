## What Is Apex Physique?

Apex Physique combines workout planning, nutrition management, and recovery-oriented tracking in a mobile-first fitness app. It is intended for people using structured gym programs who need a straightforward way to organize training, meals, supplements, and progress without staying connected all the time.

The app uses the PWA format and saves information locally in the browser. Once the initial load has finished, it remains useful for offline check-ins. Its body recomposition workflow includes workout logging, weigh-ins, macro recording, and habit-focused progress tracking.

## Core Capabilities

- Follow a 12-week push/pull/legs workout program
- Record daily food intake and macronutrients
- Use meal plans and recipes suited to preparation ahead of time
- Build shopping lists containing both food and supplements
- Time rest periods and use a workout stopwatch
- Review progress on a calendar and maintain streaks
- Keep weigh-in and strength records for longer-term analysis
- Continue using the PWA offline after its first load

## Using the App

Begin by configuring your workout schedule and recording your current body measurements. You can then document completed sessions, enter weigh-ins, monitor strength changes, and look back at progress as it accumulates.

A normal tracking session may look like this:

1. Launch Apex Physique.
2. Create or inspect the current training plan.
3. Record completed sets, rest periods, and workout sessions.
4. Enter the day's meals, macros, and supplement items.
5. Use the calendar and streak screens to evaluate consistency.

The interface is intended for fast entries during gym visits, meal preparation, and routine daily check-ins.

## Data and Configuration

Apex Physique uses the browser's `localStorage` for application data.

Stored information may include:

- Workout and training records
- Progress measurements and entries
- Meal and macro information
- Shopping list contents
- Preferences and notes from sessions

To start over, remove the site's stored data through your browser settings, then reload the application.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I use Apex Physique when I am offline?**  
Yes. After the first load, the PWA supports offline use.

**How does the app save my information?**  
Your data is stored locally in the browser through `localStorage`.

**Is the app suitable for phones?**  
Yes. Apex Physique is a mobile web app designed for phone-based fitness tracking.

**How can I install an updated build?**  
Replace the files on the deployed site with the newer build, then reload the app in your browser.

**What should I do if my information appears to be missing or reset?**  
Verify that the browser's site data was not deleted. Reload the app, and manually restore your routine if necessary.
